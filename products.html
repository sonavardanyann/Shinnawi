The Art of Wear

Sona’s 3-Step Lip Ritual

How to achieve 12-hour plush velvet comfort and clean editorial contours.

01

Condition & Blot

Begin with clean, smooth lips. Dab a whisper of balm or face oil, allow 2 minutes to soak in, then gently blot excess moisture with tissue for optimal pigment adhesion.

02

Sculpt the Arch

Use the angled, teardrop-shaped tip of your Shinnawi lipstick bullet to precisely define the cupid's bow and lower lip perimeter without requiring lip liner.

03

Glide & Press

Glide the flat facet across your lips in one smooth stroke. Gently press your lips together to activate the heat-responsive botanical oils for a weightless, cashmere cloud feel.
