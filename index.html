The Creator's Vision

A Letter From Sona

I started Shinnawi because I was exhausted by conventional lipsticks that dried out by midday or wore off unevenly. I spent three years working side-by-side with master chemists in France and Italy to achieve something rare:

A lipstick that feels like an airy silk balm, yet deposits full-spectrum color that holds its vibrancy through dinners, conversations, and long evenings. Every single shade in this 6-piece capsule was mixed, tested, and refined by hand until it was utterly transcendent.

Shinnawi is my love letter to unapologetic confidence and timeless feminine allure. I hope you cherish wearing them as much as I loved bringing them to life.

Sona Vardanyan
Founder & Formulator
