Shinnawi lipsticks are hand-poured in boutique European laboratories using ultra-fine micronized pigments suspended in organic botanical lipids (camellia seed, marula, and plant squalane). Unlike traditional matte lipsticks that dehydrate lips, our formulas create a breathable, weightless veil that softens lips while offering up to 12 hours of color fidelity.
